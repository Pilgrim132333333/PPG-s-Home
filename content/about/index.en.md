    <span>02</span>
    <div>
      <p class="about-kicker">How I work</p>
      <h2 id="about-method-en">My working principles</h2>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="about-principles">
    <article>
      <strong>01</strong>
      <h3>Build the smallest testable path first</h3>
      <p>For a large system, I begin with a minimal implementation and a trusted reference before adding optimization. Correctness is not a patch after performance; it is where optimization starts.</p>
    </article>
    <article>
      <strong>02</strong>
      <h3>Follow problems across abstractions</h3>
      <p>An anomaly may originate in the math, tensor layout, operator, cache, or scheduler. I like following the data downward until every assumption can be explained.</p>
    </article>
    <article>
      <strong>03</strong>
      <h3>Turn the process into knowledge</h3>
      <p>I record design choices, failure causes, and experimental boundaries. Writing exposes what I really understand and makes the next iteration faster.</p>
    </article>
  </div>
</section>
